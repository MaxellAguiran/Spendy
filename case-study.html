Anonymous engagement context

The work began with a fixed-budget decision.

An anonymous e-commerce advertiser needed a practical way to decide how the same monthly budget should be split across recurring advertising choices.

What the engagement needed

One action and one amount for every relevant setup.

The deliverable had to make an increase, keep, reduce, or cut decision easy to review, explain, and reconcile back to a fixed total.

What is not published

No client-account data appears here.

The anonymous engagement establishes the decision problem and delivery format. It does not establish the numerical values in the public demonstration.

Limitations

The result is useful context, not a promise.

  • It is a historical simulation across 12 later periods and 432 of 432 setup-period decisions.
  • The monthly budget and contribution-margin assumption are illustrative.
  • It does not establish realized client savings, future performance, or a live campaign outcome.
  • The anonymous client’s identity and private records remain withheld.
