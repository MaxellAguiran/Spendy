Business: CX Outsourcing With An AI Pivot

IBEX Limited (NASDAQ: IBEX) provides customer experience outsourcing through roughly 30 delivery centers. Approximately 97% of its on-site delivery capacity is located in lower-cost markets such as the Philippines, Jamaica, and Nicaragua, while about 96% of revenue comes from customers in the United States. That structure has historically supported the labor-arbitrage economics of the business.

The company is now moving beyond a purely labor-based model. Its Wave iX platform includes virtual-agent, translation, and agent-assistance capabilities under the company's "BPO 2.0" strategy: automation handles high-volume, lower-complexity interactions, while human agents focus on higher-value cases. IBEX also announced a May 2026 partnership with Sierra to integrate conversational AI agents into its customer-experience offering.

The central analytical limitation is disclosure. IBEX does not separately report Wave iX revenue, so investors cannot yet isolate the contribution from AI-enabled products or determine whether automation is expanding the addressable market faster than it cannibalizes traditional seats. Strong growth in HealthTech and Technology supports the case that the mix is improving, but segment growth alone is not proof of monetized AI revenue.

Financial Performance: The Growth Is Real

Revenue increased from $492.9 million in fiscal 2022 to $558.3 million in fiscal 2025, a compound annual growth rate of approximately 4.3%. Diluted earnings per share more than doubled over the same period, from $1.15 to $2.36, reflecting operating leverage and share repurchases.

Momentum accelerated in the third quarter of fiscal 2026. Revenue reached $164.4 million, up 16.8% year over year, while diluted EPS increased 21.9% to $0.89. The operating margin was 9.8% and the adjusted EBITDA margin was 13.4%. Management subsequently raised full-year revenue guidance from $620-$630 million to $638-$642 million and adjusted EBITDA guidance from $80-$82 million to $82-$84 million.

Peer Comparison: The Discount Has Disappeared

A leverage-neutral comparison no longer shows IBEX as clearly inexpensive. At approximately 7.0x trailing EV/EBITDA, the company trades above the 6.4x median for Concentrix, TaskUs, Genpact, and ExlService. TaskUs and Concentrix trade below IBEX, while Genpact and ExlService trade above it. ExlService commands the highest multiple because of its analytics-heavy mix.

The same conclusion appears on forward earnings. At $36.40 and a fiscal-year estimate of $3.66 per share, IBEX trades at roughly 9.9x forward earnings, approximately 19% above the peer median of 8.3x. The market is now paying for IBEX's superior growth and balance-sheet quality rather than ignoring them.

Balance Sheet And Capital Allocation

IBEX has the cleanest balance sheet in the group. At March 31, 2026, total assets were approximately $297.2 million, shareholders' equity was $160.8 million, and goodwill was only $11.8 million. The limited goodwill balance suggests that most of the company's growth has been organic rather than acquisition-driven.

Funded leverage is effectively negligible. IBEX reported roughly $1.4 million of funded lease obligations, no other long-term funded debt, $15.4 million of cash, and an undrawn $75 million revolver. This gives the company considerable flexibility, but the pace of repurchases deserves scrutiny. In fiscal 2025, IBEX spent approximately $78 million on share repurchases while generating about $45.7 million of operating cash flow and spending $18.4 million on capital expenditures. Free cash flow was therefore about $27.3 million, meaning the buyback exceeded internally generated free cash flow and drew on balance-sheet capacity. A further $20 million authorization announced in May 2026 is manageable, but it should be evaluated against the prevailing valuation.

Valuation: Fairly Priced For Its Growth

A forward discounted-cash-flow model supports a value near $40 per share. The model assumes free cash flow rises from approximately $30 million in fiscal 2026 to roughly $42 million in fiscal 2030, uses a 10% discount rate, and applies a 3% terminal growth rate. Those assumptions produce an estimated enterprise value of about $518 million. Adding approximately $15 million of net cash yields an equity value near $533 million, or roughly $40 per diluted share using 13.4 million shares.

A forward multiple approach produces a similar result near $41 per share. The agreement between the two methods is reassuring, but it also shows that the earlier mispricing has narrowed. The base case offers only about 10% upside from $36.40.

Risks And What Would Change The Rating

AI is both the opportunity and the principal risk. If automated agents replace more labor revenue than Wave iX and related products create, growth and margins could disappoint. Conversely, separate disclosure of material AI revenue would strengthen the bull case and could justify a higher multiple.

Client concentration remains relevant because the loss of a large account could materially affect revenue. Geographic concentration also exposes the operating network to political instability, natural disasters, infrastructure interruptions, and labor-market disruption in key delivery countries.

Tax expense may also rise if IBEX falls within Bermuda's corporate income-tax regime for multinational groups that exceed the applicable consolidated-revenue threshold. The timing and magnitude remain uncertain, but the issue should be incorporated into longer-term margin assumptions.

Conclusion

IBEX is a high-quality operator with credible growth, improving margins, minimal funded debt, and a sensible strategy for incorporating AI into customer-experience delivery. The problem is no longer the company; it is the price. At $36.40, the stock trades at a modest peer premium and offers an inverted base-case reward-to-risk profile.

I would upgrade the shares if IBEX begins disclosing verifiable AI-related revenue or if the stock falls into the high-$20s without a deterioration in fundamentals. Until then, the appropriate rating is Hold with a 12-month target of $40.
