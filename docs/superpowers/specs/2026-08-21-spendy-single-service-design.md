# Spendy Single-Service Design

## Purpose

Spendy is a productized machine-learning service for marketing agencies. It
forecasts the next-month break-even position of active ads and turns an approved
fixed budget into an exact per-ad allocation.

## Public promise

> Every ad gets a decision. Every dollar gets a job.

The site explains the forecast, later-period comparison, fixed-budget
reconciliation, and withholding rule without claiming advertising performance.

## Brand system

- Name: **Spendy**
- Logo: compact signal-and-flow mark, not a mascot
- Tone: crisp, evidence-led, direct, and operational
- Palette: nearly black green, electric lime, and ultraviolet
- Public surfaces: no personal founder identity, equity research, broad
  analytics, or customer-retention offer

## Active routes

- `/` — landing page and checked report preview
- `/dragon-analytics.html` — preserved legacy URL, now the Spendy method page
- `/labs/monthly-ad-report.html` — generated, checked sample report

All other previously public routes are short noindex retirement pages that point
to the method and sample report only.

## Evidence behavior

The sample report uses synthetic data. Favorable values render only after the
evidence artifact passes its checks. Recommendations are estimates, never a
performance guarantee.
