Approach

Analytical depth without ornamental complexity.

The point of a model is not to make the article look quantitative. It is to expose what the market price requires and where the thesis stops working.

Read primary disclosures

Company filings and releases take priority over summaries.

Reverse the valuation

Ask what the current price already assumes.

Build scenarios

Bear, base, and bull cases make uncertainty visible.

Name the falsifier

A thesis without a failure condition is not testable.

Separate fact from estimate

Reported evidence, author assumptions, and implications stay distinct.
