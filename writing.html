Approach

Analytical depth, without unnecessary complexity.

I'm Maxell Agustin Aguiran, an independent equity researcher and finance student. My work centres on company-specific investment theses: peer comparison, discounted-cash-flow and enterprise-value frameworks, capital allocation, and the risks actually linked to the thesis rather than a generic list.

These samples show how I test market expectations through reverse valuation — asking what the current price already assumes, and whether the disclosures support it. Every name carries a bull, base and bear case with the reward-to-risk stated explicitly, because a target price without a downside case is only half an argument.

I also run Dragon Analytics, applying the same evidence-first discipline to operating decisions rather than investment ones.
