How I work

Serious research, without the confusing words.

I'm Maxell Agustin Aguiran, an independent researcher and finance student. I look at one company at a time: how it actually makes money, how it compares to similar companies, what it might be worth, and what could go wrong.

These reports show how I work. I read the original company documents rather than other people's summaries. I test whether today's share price already assumes a perfect future. And I always give a best case, a middle case, and a worst case — because nobody knows exactly what will happen, and pretending otherwise is not honest.

I also run Dragon Analytics, where I do the same kind of careful work for businesses making everyday decisions instead of investment ones.
