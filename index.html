How I work

A reader-first process for subjects where precision matters.

Finance content needs a point of view without blurring what the evidence can support. My approach keeps the line visible.

  1. 01

    Start with the reader’s decision

    I identify the question behind the keyword or brief, then define what the piece should help the reader understand.

  2. 02

    Build from dependable sources

    For company and investing topics, that means starting with filings, regulators, and other primary material before adding interpretation.

  3. 03

    Write for clarity and search intent

    Logical headings, direct explanations, and useful context make the content easier to find, scan, and trust.

  4. 04

    Deliver publish-ready work

    Every draft is edited for structure, tone, citations, and the claims it makes, so review time stays focused on real editorial decisions.
