About Maxell

Forecasting taught me to respect what the evidence cannot say.

My interest in prediction began in markets and developed through quantitative research, sports forecasting, company analysis, and business modeling. Across those settings, the lesson has been consistent: complexity is useful only when it beats a sensible alternative on evidence it has not already seen.

Dragon Analytics brings that discipline to practical business decisions.

Real baselineA transparent alternative gets the first chance to win.
Unseen evidenceLater periods or untouched accounts stay out of fitting.
Explicit limitsUncertainty and failure conditions are part of the result.
