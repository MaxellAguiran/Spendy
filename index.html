The method

Less guessing. More accountable spend.

A single monthly decision system built for marketing agencies.

  1. 01

    Connect the history.

    Share Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, Shopify or equivalent sales data, your break-even definition, and the fixed budget for the coming month.

  2. 02

    Forecast, then check.

    The machine-learning model estimates next-month break-even position for each ad and is checked against a simple comparison using later data it did not learn from.

  3. 03

    Allocate with intent.

    Receive Cut, Reduce, Keep, or Increase for every ad, an exact spend amount, forecast range, assumptions, and limits.

The guardrail

No stronger signal means no spend recommendation.

Spendy withholds the allocation when the available history is unreliable or the forecast does not improve on a simple comparison. A model only earns the right to influence budget when it proves more useful than the obvious answer.
