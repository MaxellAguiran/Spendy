A little about me

Two jobs, one simple idea.

My name is Maxell Agustin Aguiran. Whether I am writing a report or building a model, I work the same way: find the real numbers, check whether the popular story is actually true, and then say plainly what I found.

I try hard to write in simple English. Many of the people I work with do not speak English as their first language, and that should never be a reason to feel confused by your own business data. If something I write is unclear, tell me and I will explain it a different way.

If you have a decision to make and you already have the data for it, that is exactly the kind of problem I enjoy. Come say hello.
