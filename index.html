Questions that stop a purchase

What happens when the evidence is weak?

“No plan yet” is the result. You receive the completed diagnostic PDF and workbook showing the checks performed, current allocation, evidence gaps, and what a future plan would require.

Which platforms and data can qualify?

Two platforms can qualify when their exported files contain sufficient compatible information. Shopify inputs are aggregate business values such as revenue, orders, refunds, and margin assumptions.

What is excluded?

The fixed audit does not include campaign management, implementation, a follow-up call, recurring service, credentials, customer-level exports, or work above the stated scope.

When does delivery start?

Within three business days after the requested exports are accepted as complete and usable—not when the fit check, invoice payment, or incomplete files are received.
