The prediction arc

A long apprenticeship in uncertainty.

Different domains, one recurring question: what evidence would justify changing a decision before the outcome is known?

Markets

Currency markets first taught me that a precise number can still be a fragile forecast—and that timing matters as much as direction.

Quantitative research

Systematic research sharpened the discipline: clean lineage, chronological tests, real baselines, and an honest refusal when evidence is incomplete.

Sports forecasting

Football prediction made calibration tangible. A model can rank outcomes while still being poorly suited to a decision at the available threshold.

Business decisions

Dragon Analytics applies that accumulated discipline to budgets, retention risk, and adjacent forecasting questions people can act on.
