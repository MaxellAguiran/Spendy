Questions people ask

Does the audit guarantee ROAS or profit?

No. It uses past performance to support a budget decision. It cannot guarantee a future return, revenue, or profit outcome.

What happens when the evidence is weak?

“No plan yet” is the result. You still receive the completed diagnostic PDF and workbook showing the checks performed, the current allocation, the evidence gaps, and what would be needed for a future plan.

Which platforms can qualify?

Any two platforms may qualify if their exported files contain sufficient compatible information. Compatibility is confirmed during fit review.

What Shopify information is needed?

Aggregate business inputs such as revenue, orders, refunds, and margin assumptions. Do not send customer-level exports or other unnecessary personal data.

What happens above 100 ads?

The fixed offer does not apply. Spendy will confirm whether a separate quote makes sense before any invoice is sent.
