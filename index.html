How the report works

From ad exports to next month's exact spend plan.

One report answers one practical question: what should happen to every ad in the next monthly budget?

  1. 01

    Send the history and the budget.

    Provide Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, or similar exports; Shopify or equivalent sales data; your break-even definition; and the fixed total budget for next month.

  2. 02

    I forecast and check.

    I estimate which ads may move below or above break-even, then compare the forecast with a simple answer using later data it did not learn from.

  3. 03

    Receive the monthly plan.

    You get Cut, Reduce, Keep, or Increase for every ad; an exact spend amount for each one; forecast ranges; assumptions; limits; and the supporting files.

The work needs consistent ad identity, dated spend and sales outcomes, and enough history to save a later period for an honest check.
