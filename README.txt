CONTACT FORM
The form on dragon-analytics.html opens the visitor's email client with
the message drafted. The destination is set in ONE place: the line
reading   var TO = '...'   in the <script> block at the bottom of
dragon-analytics.html.

It needs no signup and no server, but it does require the visitor to have
an email client configured. To remove that dependency later, replace the
script with a hosted form endpoint.

HOUSE STYLE
Plain language, but not simplified content. Define a term once, keep
sentences short, and let the rigour show in the method rather than in
vocabulary. Many clients use English as a second language; that is a
reason for clarity, not for talking down.

The example engagements on dragon-analytics.html are explicitly labelled
as illustrative. Replace them with real figures only when there are real
client results to cite.
