Test design

Untouched accounts and calibrated risk.

The model is fitted on one cohort and evaluated once on another. The comparison uses probabilistic loss, not a class-imbalance-friendly accuracy headline.

Account histories

Tenure, 90-day usage change, communication gap, billing change, delivered-performance index, support contacts, monthly revenue, churn outcome, and lead time are generated with a fixed seed.

Held-out cohort

The first 4,000 synthetic accounts are available for fitting. The final 1,000 remain untouched until scoring.

Baseline

Every held-out account receives the development cohort’s constant churn rate. Its Brier score and log loss are scored on the same accounts as the model.

Decision metrics

Calibration, top-decile precision and lift, exposure-weighted revenue at risk, and lead time connect probability quality to a finite outreach decision.

Intervention assumptions & costs

Ranking risk does not retain a customer.

  • Discrimination does not prove an intervention will work.
  • Revenue at risk is exposure, not saved or realised revenue.
  • False positives consume service capacity and can create fatigue.
  • Calibration must be rechecked after material operational changes.
