Technical definition

Mean absolute error (MAE) is the average absolute difference between forecast and observed revenue-to-spend ratio during the later 30-day check. Lower is better. The regularized regression uses trend, weekly and monthly patterns, spend saturation, platform, and generated ad identity.

Limits

What this generated report does not prove.

  • Attributed sales do not prove that ad spend caused the result.
  • A generated forecast does not guarantee future performance.
  • The example does not support spending outside the generated range.
  • Platform, offer, tracking, price, inventory, and market changes can break the pattern.
