Test design

Chronology before cleverness.

Later revenue never enters fitting. Both model and baseline face the same untouched weeks.

Dataset

Weekly search, social, email, and partnership spend are combined with annual seasonality, organic demand, channel-specific carryover, saturating response, and seeded noise.

Development / holdout

Weeks 1–130 are available for fitting. Weeks 131–156 are held out in time order. Random shuffling is not used.

Baseline

The transparent baseline uses revenue from the same week one year earlier. It is evaluated with mean absolute error and root mean squared error.

Model

A regularized lagged-response regression uses trend, annual seasonality, channel adstock, and logged response features to express diminishing returns.

Assumptions & failure conditions

What this result cannot establish.

  • The synthetic process is more regular than a live market.
  • Attribution in a simulation does not prove real-world incrementality.
  • Response is unsupported outside the observed spend range.
  • The allocation assumes response and budget constraints stay stable.
