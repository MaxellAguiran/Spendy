Audit Terms

The fixed scope, stated plainly.

Effective 22 August 2026. These terms describe the operational scope of the €1,500 Spendy ROAS Budget Audit.

The audit

The audit is a one-off budget-allocation review for European performance marketing agencies. It reviews full available usable history for up to two compatible advertising platforms, up to 100 ads, and Shopify aggregate business inputs. Every reviewed ad is traced to an increase, keep, reduce, or cut decision at the lowest level where the platform permits budget control.

The delivered formats are a PDF decision report and a working workbook. They include proposed allocations, rationale, assumptions, limitations, and fixed-budget reconciliation.

Price, fit, and payment

The quoted audit fee is €1,500. The applicable tax treatment is confirmed in writing before invoice. A fit check is not an acceptance of work, a guarantee of a recommendation, or a request to send files. Spendy confirms fit and scope before issuing an invoice. After payment, the client sends the requested exports through the agreed private channel.

More than 100 ads, more than two platforms, unsupported exports, implementation work, re-analysis, or a changed scope requires a separate quote.

Delivery

Delivery is within three business days after all required exports are accepted as complete and usable. The delivery clock does not begin when the enquiry is submitted, the invoice is paid, or incomplete files are received.

Do not send passwords, API keys, platform login credentials, customer-level Shopify exports, or unnecessary personal data. Raw client exports are scheduled for deletion within 30 calendar days after delivery.

No-plan outcome

No plan yet is a valid audit conclusion. The audit does not guarantee ROAS, revenue, profit, or any future performance outcome. When no defensible reallocation plan exists, the €1,500 fee covers the completed audit and the client receives a PDF and workbook documenting the checks performed, current allocation, evidence gaps, and what would be needed for a future plan.

Corrections and exclusions

Report a Spendy clerical or calculation error within 14 calendar days after delivery. Spendy will correct a verified error without an additional fee. New data, changed assumptions, interpretation calls, implementation work, and re-analysis are not corrections and require separate agreement.

No follow-up call, campaign-management work, implementation assistance, or ongoing service is included in the fixed audit.

Operational status

These terms describe the current service workflow. They do not replace legal, tax, accounting, or advertising-platform advice. The service owner must review this operational wording, the data-processing workflow, and local legal requirements before a public release or business-structure change.
