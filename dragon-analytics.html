Model channel response with carryover and diminishing return, compare current and constrained allocations, and test the forecast on later weeks the model did not fit.

Handover: reproducible data transformation, model evidence, response curves, allocation scenarios, and documented failure conditions.

Concise FAQ

Questions worth answering before the work starts.

What information do you need?

Enough historical, point-in-time data to recreate what was knowable when each decision would have been made. The exact fields depend on the decision.

How much data is enough?

There is no universal row count. Frequency, regime changes, event rarity, seasonality, and the size of the intended holdout matter more than a headline total.

What if the baseline wins?

The simpler baseline remains the recommended instrument. A complex model is not a deliverable merely because it was built.

Can you guarantee an outcome?

No. Forecast quality can be evaluated; realised business outcomes also depend on execution, market conditions, intervention design, and factors outside a model.

Do you work with sensitive data?

Data handling, minimisation, access, and retention must be agreed before transfer. This static website collects no project data.

Will my team be able to use the result?

That is an explicit goal of the handover: understandable inputs, repeatable scoring or scenarios, documented thresholds, and known failure conditions.

Do you take adjacent forecasting projects?

Sometimes. The project still needs a concrete decision, evaluation design, and enough history to separate signal from a persuasive story.

How do we begin?

Send a short email describing the decision, timing, available history, and operational constraint. No long form or platform account is required.
