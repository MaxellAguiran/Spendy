FAQ

Before the report begins.

What data is required?

Dated ad-level spend and performance exports, Shopify or equivalent sales results, stable ad identifiers, your break-even definition, and the fixed total budget for the coming month.

How much history is useful?

There is no honest universal minimum. The history needs to cover enough repeated conditions to learn from earlier periods while protecting a later period for a separate check.

How is break-even defined?

We agree the business definition before the report. It must reflect the costs and sales measure your agency actually uses rather than a generic number copied from the sample.

What happens if the simple comparison wins?

No ad actions or recommended spend plan are released. A more complicated forecast does not earn a place merely because it was built.

How are confidential exports handled?

The first email is only for scoping. This website has no upload form and collects no project data. Handling terms for confidential files are agreed separately before files are shared.
