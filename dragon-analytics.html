How the work runs

Four steps from decision to handover.

Scope and commercial terms are agreed only after the decision and available evidence are understood. No outcome guarantee is implied.

  1. 01Frame

    Name the action, timing, capacity, and cost of error.

  2. 02Audit

    Check point-in-time data, leakage risk, and the baseline.

  3. 03Validate

    Fit on development data and test on later or untouched evidence.

  4. 04Handover

    Deliver the operating view, documentation, uncertainty, and recheck conditions.

A good fit

  • A recurring decision with meaningful consequences.
  • Historical information that existed before the outcome.
  • A team able to act on the result.

Not a good fit

  • A request for guaranteed outcomes.
  • No credible baseline or holdout.
  • A model wanted only because data exists.

FAQ

Before the work starts.

What information do you need?

Enough historical, point-in-time data to recreate what was knowable when each decision would have been made. The exact fields depend on the decision.

How much data is enough?

There is no universal row count. Frequency, regime changes, event rarity, seasonality, and the size of the intended holdout matter more than a headline total.

What if the baseline wins?

The simpler baseline remains the recommended instrument. A complex model is not a deliverable merely because it was built.

Can you guarantee an outcome?

No. Forecast quality can be evaluated; realised business outcomes also depend on execution, market conditions, intervention design, and factors outside a model.
