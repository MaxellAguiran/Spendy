How Spendy stays honest

It checks itself before showing a plan.

Spendy uses machine learning to look for useful patterns. Then it checks its answer against a simple way of looking at the same past information. If Spendy is not clearly more helpful, it does not show a budget plan.

Questions people ask
What do I need to send?

Past ad spend, what happened after the ads ran, what a good result looks like for you, and next month's total budget.

What if Spendy cannot make a helpful plan?

It says “no plan yet.” We do not make up an answer just because you asked for one.

Will the plan guarantee sales?

No. It is a helpful estimate based on the past, not a promise about the future.
