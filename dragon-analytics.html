What comes in

Ad history, sales signals, break-even, and the fixed budget.

Enough consistent, dated information to learn from earlier periods and check a later period honestly.

Campaign history
Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, or equivalent exports with stable ad identities and spend records.
Sales outcomes
Shopify or comparable dated sales results that can be connected responsibly to the advertising history.
Break-even
The business definition of break-even the team already uses for campaign decisions.
Fixed budget
The exact total that needs to be allocated across next month’s active ads.
What comes out

A clear next move and an exact spend number for every ad.

Forecast uncertainty and operational allocation are stated separately.

Forecast
A next-month range relative to break-even, never a promise of results.
Action
Cut, Reduce, Keep, or Increase for every active ad in scope.
Allocation
An exact per-ad spend recommendation that reconciles to the fixed budget in whole cents.
Handover
The later-period check, assumptions, limits, and supporting files needed to inspect the decision.
When the answer is no

No stronger forecast means no Spendy allocation.

The decision is withheld if the history is incomplete, ad identity changed, tracking became unreliable, or the model fails to improve on a simple comparison. More complicated does not automatically mean more useful.

FAQ
What data is required?

Dated ad-level spend and outcome exports, stable ad identifiers, a break-even definition, and the fixed total budget.

What happens if the model is not stronger?

The allocation is withheld. There is no benefit in manufacturing a more complex answer.

Does the forecast guarantee results?

No. It is an estimate of a future period and does not prove that advertising spend caused the result.
