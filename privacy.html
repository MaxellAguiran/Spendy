Privacy

Plain-language handling of an audit enquiry.

Effective 22 August 2026. This page describes the current, static-site workflow for the Spendy ROAS Budget Audit.

What the public page collects

The qualification form asks for your name, work email, company or team, website, team type, advertising platforms, spend range, ad-count range, Shopify use, primary outcome, and the budget decision you need to make.

The public qualification form prepares an email in your mail app. The static website does not upload the form, create a client account, or store its form fields in a database.

What to send later

Only after Spendy confirms fit and payment should you send the requested advertising exports and Shopify aggregate business inputs by email. Use aggregate values such as revenue, orders, refunds, and margin assumptions where possible.

Do not send passwords, API keys, platform login credentials, customer-level Shopify exports, or other unnecessary personal data. If information is not required for the allocation audit, leave it out.

Retention and deletion

Raw client exports are scheduled for deletion within 30 calendar days after delivery. Qualification messages and audit correspondence are retained only as needed to respond, deliver the agreed work, address a correction request, or meet applicable business-record obligations.

You may request access or deletion by emailing maxell.aguiran@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained where a legal or accounting obligation applies.

Cookies and analytics

The current Spendy site does not use analytics, advertising pixels, a customer relationship manager, or a third-party form endpoint. If that changes, this page will be updated before the new processing is used.

Scope of this notice

This is operational information for the current service workflow, not a claim of a particular legal certification or compliance status. If the service structure changes, the policy must be reviewed and updated before publication.
