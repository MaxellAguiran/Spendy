Privacy

Plain-language handling of a fit check and audit.

Effective 22 August 2026. This page describes the intended public workflow for the Spendy ROAS Budget Audit before a production form or analytics configuration is enabled.

What the fit check collects

The public fit check asks for business email, agency or company, website, monthly ad-spend band, platforms, Shopify use, ad-count band, and an optional description of the allocation decision. It also records an acknowledgement that no sensitive material should be submitted.

It does not ask for a personal name, files, passwords, API keys, platform credentials, or customer-level data. Please do not add those items in the optional text field.

How the form is handled

The source package uses a placeholder endpoint and blocks submission in local or unconfigured previews. A configured production release sends only the approved fit-check fields to a Formspree endpoint selected by the service owner; it does not create a public client account or invite file uploads.

The Formspree project settings, recipient access, data-processing terms, and retention settings must be reviewed by the service owner before a real form ID is supplied. Until then, the form is intentionally not a live submission channel.

What to send later

Only after Spendy confirms fit, scope, and payment should you send the requested advertising exports and Shopify aggregate business inputs through the agreed private channel. Use aggregate values such as revenue, orders, refunds, and margin assumptions where possible.

Do not send passwords, API keys, platform login credentials, customer-level Shopify exports, or other unnecessary personal data. If information is not required for the allocation audit, leave it out.

Retention and deletion

Fit checks that do not become an agreed engagement are scheduled for deletion within 90 calendar days. Raw client exports are scheduled for deletion within 30 calendar days after delivery. Audit correspondence is retained only as needed to respond, deliver the agreed work, address a correction request, or meet applicable business-record obligations.

You may request access or deletion by emailing maxell.aguiran@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained where a legal or accounting obligation applies.

Limited analytics

When a production release has an owner-provided domain configuration, it may load Plausible analytics for page views and the approved conversion events shown in the site code: CTA clicks, fit-check start, fit-check submit, sample-report open, methodology open, and FAQ open. The event payload allows only event name, page type, and CTA location. It sends no fit-check field values, email address, agency, website, decision text, files, or customer data.

The site does not add advertising pixels or use analytics to build an audience from form answers. Plausible is absent from the source and local preview package until a production domain is deliberately configured.

Scope of this notice

This is operational information for the current service workflow, not a claim of a particular legal certification or compliance status. The service owner must review the form processor, analytics configuration, tax position, and local legal requirements before public release.
