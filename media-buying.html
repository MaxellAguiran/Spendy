Spendy

A portfolio project for one narrow operating problem.

Instead of treating ROAS as a headline metric, the work connects a proposed budget move to the evidence needed to support it.

Client work

Bold Decisions

Worked with Bold Decisions, an independent performance marketing firm, on Meta Ads. Their creative team created the ads while I monitored performance, flagged declines, optimized budget and revenue decisions, and shared the learning back into the creative loop. Specific results are private.

02 · Frame the decision

Start with a fixed budget and a real choice.

Define whether the next action is to increase, hold, reduce, or cut an allocation before working backward to the evidence.

03 · Test the evidence

Make uncertainty visible.

Use explicit checks, forecast ranges, and decision thresholds rather than presenting an attractive number as a settled fact.
